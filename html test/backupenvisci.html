Storm Surge Protocol
1
Evacuate Coastal Areas Immediately

If coastal water levels recede abnormally or storm surge alert Level 2 is issued, move to high ground right away.

2
Shut Off Main Utilities

Disconnect main electrical breakers and gas tanks to prevent electrocution or post-storm fires.

3
Seek High Elevation Structures

If trapped, move to the 2nd floor or reinforced concrete concrete structure like Southern Samar National Comprehensive HS.

First Aid Fundamentals
1
Severe Bleeding

Apply firm, direct pressure on the wound using a clean cloth or bandage. Keep pressure steady for at least 10 minutes.

2
Water Disinfection

If boiling is impossible, add 2 drops of unscented household bleach per 1 liter of water and let sit for 30 minutes before drinking.
